About Colorido

Where Talent Meets Celebration

COLORIDO 2K26 is the flagship national-level cultural and sports festival hosted by R.V.R. & J.C. College of Engineering, Guntur. It brings together students from across the country to celebrate creativity, athleticism, and youthful energy.

From electrifying dance performances to nail-biting sports finals, from artistic showcases to literary brilliance — COLORIDO is a platform where talent takes center stage and memories are made that last a lifetime.
